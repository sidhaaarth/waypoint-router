What the router does

Small model first

Nemotron Nano reads the message and does most of the work. If it isn't sure, the question goes up to Super, and to Ultra only when really needed. Its own word-by-word certainty decides, not a guess made in advance.

Checks before words

Plain code copies order numbers and emails exactly. Your store's records decide who the customer is. Your written rules decide what's allowed. The model only writes the reply once the facts are settled.

Every decision on record

Each reply keeps a record: which model did which step, how sure it was, what the store said, what it cost. Customer details stay out of it unless you opt in.
