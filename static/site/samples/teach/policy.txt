Returns: unworn items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Card disputes: a customer can dispute a card payment within 120 days; we freeze the card first if fraud is suspected.
Claims: home insurance claims under $1,000 are paid within 5 working days once photos and a receipt are received.
Broadband outages: if service is down for more than 24 hours, the customer gets one day's credit for each day without service.
Subscriptions: annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 14 days of renewal; monthly plans are not refunded.
Flight changes: free date changes up to 24 hours before departure; after that a $75 change fee applies.
Energy bills: customers on a payment plan who miss two payments are contacted by a person, never cut off automatically.
Course withdrawals: students who withdraw within the first 2 weeks get a full tuition refund; after week 4, no refund.
Appointments: cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are charged $40, waived once per patient per year.
Deliveries: late food orders (over 30 minutes past the promised time) get the delivery fee refunded automatically.
