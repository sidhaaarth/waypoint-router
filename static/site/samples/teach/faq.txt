Q: Do you ship abroad?
A: Yes, to 30 countries. International delivery takes 5–10 working days and is tracked.

Q: How long does a bank transfer take?
A: Transfers to other UK banks usually arrive within 2 hours. International transfers take 1–3 working days.

Q: Does my car insurance cover a rental car?
A: Only if you added rental cover to your policy. You can check under Policy details > Extras in the app.

Q: Can I keep my phone number if I switch to you?
A: Yes. Text PAC to your old provider, then give us the code at checkout. The switch happens within one working day.

Q: How do I reset two-factor authentication?
A: Ask your workspace admin to reset it from Admin > Users. If you are the only admin, contact support with your account ID.

Q: How much baggage can I bring?
A: Economy fares include one cabin bag up to 10 kg. Checked bags can be added in Manage booking.

Q: How do I submit a meter reading?
A: In the app under Account > Meter readings, or by replying to our monthly reminder text with the number.

Q: When are exam results published?
A: Results are published in the student portal 3 weeks after the last exam of the term.

Q: Can I see a doctor on the same day?
A: Same-day slots open at 8 am each weekday. Book online or call the front desk.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
A: Yes, until the restaurant accepts the order. After that, contact us in the app and we will try to help.
